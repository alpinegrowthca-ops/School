## Reference-ad blueprint

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, photoreal handheld phone footage, native TikTok look, captions only.

- **Hook:** a fake near-miss VFX gag. A lorry's impossible box tower passes under a bridge and one box flies at the filmer. The first caption is a deadpan complaint. For DESIRE it opens with “Nicotine-free”, so the dossier's first-words rule and the joke land together.

- **Pacing:** 11 beats in about 15 s. Chaotic 0.5–1 s gag beats for 5 s, then 1–2.5 s product beats. The hard music drop lands on the product reveal at 0:05.0.

- **Framing and motion:** handheld POV throughout. Overpass wide shot, top-down onto the box, close unboxing, one low-angle hero shot, desk-level demo, top-down finish.

- **Recurring devices:** the plain cardboard box links the gag to the delivery. The demo is shown as play: the RC car's drift becomes the tins sliding and spinning into a row, and the controller-in-hand becomes the one-handed tin flick.

- **Roles:** the product is the only hero. The creator is hands and sneakers only. Nobody talks.

- **Captions:** one per phase, centered white TikTok-style text with emoji. Joke → what it is → benefit → the three flavours → “Tap Shop Now” and 18+.

- **Ending:** a hard cut with no end card, so the ad loops.
