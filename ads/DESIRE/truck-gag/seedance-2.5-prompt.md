# DESIRE “Almost took me out”: Seedance 2.5 master prompt (16 s, 9:16, voiceover + audio)

One prompt for the whole ad. It follows the 11-clip storyboard in `storyboard.md`, merged into 10 continuous shots, with a spoken voiceover added.

## Settings

- **Duration:** 16 s. If your platform only offers fixed lengths, pick the next one up; the final shot is written to hold.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16. **Resolution:** 1080p or higher. **Audio:** ON (native generation).
- **References:** upload a real photo of each tin (Cool Mint, Watermelon Ice, Mixed Berry) and one pouch photo. Tag them however your platform does it (for example `@image1`), and change the tags in the prompt to match. With no photos, delete the REFERENCES block and the written descriptions do the work.
- **Captions:** leave them out of the generation. AI text wobbles. Add the caption track at the bottom of this file in CapCut or TikTok.

## The prompt (copy everything in the box)

```text
FORMAT
A 16-second vertical 9:16 photorealistic multi-shot video with native synchronized audio: one off-screen voiceover, live ambience, sound effects and a music track. It must look like a real, unpolished TikTok UGC video filmed on a smartphone: handheld, natural available light, slight motion blur on fast moves, true-to-life colours, no cinematic grading, no film grain, no lens flares, no slow motion. Ten shots joined by hard cuts on the beat. No transitions, fades, zooms-between-shots or title cards.

REFERENCES
@image1 = the Cool Mint tin. @image2 = the Watermelon Ice tin. @image3 = the Mixed Berry tin. Match each tin's shape, finish, logo, lettering and rim colour exactly whenever it appears. @image4 = the pouch; match its size, shape and colour.

CONTINUITY LOCK (identical in every shot; never redesign)
TINS: Each tin is a slim round matte-black metal tin about the width of a palm and two centimeters tall, with a flush pressed lid, the word DESIRE in clean white uppercase sans-serif lettering centered on the lid, a small white NICOTINE FREE line beneath the logo, and a thin flavour ring around the lid edge: mint-teal for Cool Mint, watermelon-pink for Watermelon Ice, deep berry-purple for Mixed Berry. The rim colours never swap, blend or change. When open, each tin holds a neat ring of small white pouches.
POUCH: A small white rectangular oral pouch with softly rounded corners, a slightly padded smooth fleece-like surface, and no visible grounds or leaks.
HANDS: A pair of adult hands with light-olive skin, broad knuckles, short clean nails, and a plain black analog wristwatch on the left wrist. These are the only body parts of the filmer ever seen, apart from white low-top sneakers and dark jeans in shots 4 and 5. Never show a face.
LORRY: A white European cab-over lorry pulling a flatbed trailer loaded with an absurdly tall, perfectly stacked tower of identical plain brown cardboard boxes, several stories high.
SHIPPING BOX: A plain brown corrugated cardboard shipping box with one strip of clear packing tape and no printing.
MAILER: A small square matte-black mailer box with a hinged lid, lined with a charcoal-grey foam insert holding the three tins in snug circular cut-outs, with no printing inside.
LOCATION A (shots 1–5): a concrete pedestrian overpass with a weathered, rusty chain-link railing across the bottom of frame, above a wide six-lane motorway that stretches away between bare winter trees and tall streetlights under a flat grey overcast sky.
LOCATION B (shots 6–10): a glossy black wood-grain desk in a dim home office at night, with an open laptop showing a blurred spreadsheet, a low-profile black keyboard, a matte black mouse, a warm brass desk lamp, and a charcoal wall.

VOICE (one voice for the entire video)
The only voice is the filmer, speaking off-screen from behind the phone. He is never on camera, and nobody in the video lip-syncs. Voice: an adult man in his late twenties with a mid-low pitch, a slightly raspy texture, a relaxed neutral North American accent, and a casual deadpan UGC delivery. He sounds natural and unscripted, close to the phone mic. The emotion changes by shot as noted below, but the pitch, accent, age and timbre never change. He speaks ONLY the exact quoted lines below, in order, with no added words, ad-libs or extra dialogue.

TIMELINE

[0:00–0:02] SHOT 1. Location A. Handheld phone held at chest height looking down the motorway through the railing. The LORRY drives steadily toward the bridge in the center lane among ordinary cars, its box tower swaying slightly. Camera: static handheld with a very slight zoom-in. Mood: calm, curious.
Voiceover (amused, low-key): "Yo, look at this truck."
Sound: loud motorway roar and gusting wind hitting the phone mic; a distant lorry air-horn blast near the end. Music: none yet.

[0:02–0:03] SHOT 2. Location A. The LORRY passes directly beneath the bridge. Its tower of plain brown boxes rises past the railing and fills most of the frame like a wall of cardboard, and the top layers buckle and start spilling toward the lens. Camera: jolts backward in surprise.
Voiceover (rising panic, fast): "No, no, no—"
Sound: the motorway roar swells; a deep whoosh of displaced air; cardboard scraping concrete.

[0:03–0:04] SHOT 3. Location A. A single SHIPPING BOX breaks off the top, tumbles end over end through the grey sky over the railing, and drops straight at the lens until brown cardboard fills the frame. Camera: tilts up to follow it, then flinches.
Voiceover (shouted, genuine fright): "Whoa!"
Sound: a quick rising whoosh as the box falls; the wind drops to near-silence.

[0:04–0:05] SHOT 4. Location A, top-down POV at the footpath beside the bridge. The SHIPPING BOX lands flat on the grey concrete with a small bounce, flaps now open, revealing the closed MAILER nestled inside. The filmer's white sneakers and dark jeans are at the bottom of frame and shuffle half a step closer. Camera: slight downward handheld drift.
Voiceover (shaky exhale, deadpan): "Bro."
Sound: a solid cardboard thud on impact; muffled distant traffic; a short music riser starts under the last half-second.

[0:05–0:07] SHOT 5. Location B, close top-down. A hard cut exactly on the music drop. The HANDS hold the MAILER lid open, then lift the Cool Mint tin (@image1) out of its foam cut-out and raise it toward the lens, tilting it so the DESIRE logo and NICOTINE FREE line catch the warm lamp light. The Watermelon Ice and Mixed Berry tins stay in their cut-outs. Camera: handheld, following the lift. Mood: confident, reveal energy.
Voiceover (dry, confident, now clearly selling): "Nicotine-free caffeine pouches."
Music: HARD DROP on the first frame of this shot into a punchy, bass-heavy phonk-style instrumental (original composition, no vocals). Sound: a soft foam squeak as the tin pops free.

[0:07–0:07.5] SHOT 6. Location B. Low-angle hero close-up of the Cool Mint tin (@image1) standing on its edge on the glossy desk, the brass lamp glowing softly behind it and a sharp reflection on the desktop. A glint of lamp light slides across the lid. Camera: tiny orbit.
Voiceover (continuing): "Eighty milligrams each."
Sound: a small bright metallic ting on the glint; music at full energy.

[0:07.5–0:08.5] SHOT 7. Location B, tight close-up. One HAND flicks the Cool Mint tin (@image1) open one-handed with a crisp snap, revealing the ring of pouches; the other hand pinches out one POUCH (@image4) and lifts it up and out of frame toward the face. The mouth is never shown. Camera: static close framing.
Voiceover (steady): "Fast release."
Sound: a crisp metal tin-lid click landing exactly on a beat.

[0:08.5–0:10.5] SHOT 8. Location B, over-the-shoulder. Both HANDS type quickly and steadily on the keyboard, then give the mouse one decisive click to finish a row on the blurred spreadsheet, calm and locked in. The open Cool Mint tin sits beside the mouse under the lamp. Camera: slow push-in over the shoulder, no face visible.
Voiceover (calm, assured): "No jitters. Helps you focus."
Sound: soft, fast keyboard clatter and one mouse click, tucked under the voice.

[0:10.5–0:14] SHOT 9. Location B, low wide angle along the desk. The three closed tins (Cool Mint @image1, Watermelon Ice @image2, Mixed Berry @image3) slide in from the right edge across the glossy surface, spinning slightly like drifting cars, and come to rest perfectly lined up side by side in front of the laptop: mint-teal, watermelon-pink, berry-purple from left to right. No hands in frame. Camera: static, low.
Voiceover (playful, cheeky grin in the voice): "JK, we actually sell them. Cool Mint, Watermelon Ice, Mixed Berry."
Sound: a smooth metallic slide hiss, then three soft taps as the tins stop in a row, the last tap on a beat.

[0:14–0:16] SHOT 10. Location B, top-down POV. The three tins sit lined up in the middle of frame (mint-teal, watermelon-pink, berry-purple from left to right), with the HANDS resting at the bottom of frame. One hand reaches in and pops open the Watermelon Ice tin (@image2) in the middle of the row, then withdraws. Hold this frame to the end; no fade, so the video loops.
Voiceover (relaxed, final): "Link's below. Eighteen plus."
Sound: a crisp metal tin-lid click on the pop; the music runs straight to the last frame with no fade-out.

AUDIO MIX
The voiceover is always the loudest, clearest element: dry and close-miked, never drowned out. Duck the music about 10 dB under every spoken line. Shots 1–4 are raw phone-mic realism (wind, traffic roar, near-silence during the fall), then silence for a split second before the drop. From 0:05 the bass-heavy instrumental carries the video to the end, and the tin-lid clicks land on beats. There are no vocals in the music.

CONSTRAINTS
No on-screen text of any kind: no captions, subtitles, stickers, emojis, watermarks, UI or extra logos. The only lettering is the DESIRE and NICOTINE FREE print on the tins, which must stay sharp, correctly spelled and unwarped. Never show a face, a mouth or lips. No cigarettes, vapes, nicotine pouches, tobacco or smoking imagery. No children, teens, schools or campuses. No energy-drink brands or competitor logos. The tins keep their exact shape, size, finish and rim colours in every shot, never melt, stretch, duplicate or morph, and the three rim colours never swap. Hands have five fingers each and move naturally. Speak only the quoted voiceover lines, in order, word for word. Do not add dialogue, narration, singing or background voices.
```

## If the result needs fixing

- **Tins drift off-model:** add the tin photos as references (the biggest fix), or generate shots 5–10 separately with the tin photo as the first frame.
- **Voice changes between shots:** generate the VO with a TTS or voice tool, upload it as an audio reference, and write “use the uploaded audio as the voiceover, exactly as is” under VOICE.
- **Prompt gets truncated:** split it into two generations, 0:00–0:05 (the gag) and 0:05–0:16 (the product), each keeping the FORMAT, CONTINUITY LOCK, VOICE, AUDIO MIX and CONSTRAINTS blocks.
- **Claims:** the voiceover now *says* “Fast release. No jitters. Helps you focus.” out loud. The same flags from `script.md` apply: have proof on file for the first two, and “helps you focus” can't run in EU or UK ads.
