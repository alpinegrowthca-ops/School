## If the result needs fixing

- **A caption is misspelled:** delete the ON-SCREEN TEXT block, regenerate, and add the captions in CapCut from the table above. Or regenerate only the affected shot.
- **Tins drift off-model:** use the tin photos as references, or generate shots 5–10 separately with the tin photo as the first frame.
- **Voice changes between shots or mispronounces a word:** record the voiceover (or make it with a TTS tool) from the script table, upload it as an audio reference, and add “Use the uploaded audio as the voiceover, exactly as is” under VOICE.
- **Prompt too long:** split it into two generations, 0:00–0:05 (the gag) and 0:05–0:18 (the product), repeating FORMAT, CONTINUITY LOCK, VOICE, ON-SCREEN TEXT, AUDIO MIX and CONSTRAINTS in both.
