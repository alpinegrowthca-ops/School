**Status: DRAFT.** I wrote this script from the research dossier because no finished script was supplied. Every factual line below is traced to a dossier section. Replace or edit it freely, then re-run the `storyboard-ai-ad` skill on your version.

**Strategy, from the dossier:** lead with “Nicotine free” in the first three words (§4.5). Nod to the nicotine-pouch crossover buyer through the *same 15-pouch tin*, never a quitting claim (§4.1, §9.5). Make the villain *not knowing*, never caffeine (§7.5, §10.F). Anchor the dose to an 8.4 oz energy drink (§2.1). Give flavor its own beat (§3.1). Borrow the Walter Reed format lineage without borrowing its numbers (§8.1). Close on honest expectations (§10.C).

## End-card text (edit layer, US cut)

- DESIRE · Nicotine free · 80 mg caffeine · 80 mg L-theanine · every dose on the tin
- “For adults 18+. Not for use if pregnant or nursing.” **[VERIFY]** final label warnings
- DSHEA disclaimer: “These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.”
- **Canada cut:** add “High caffeine content” and “Do not eat on the same day as any other source of caffeine” (§9.1). **EU/UK cut:** remove lines 9–10 and any alertness or focus implication (§9.2).

## Deliberately left out (and why)

- Price, subscription discount, guarantee, scarcity: not confirmed yet (§5.3–5.4).
- Quit or cessation language and before/after nicotine visuals: this could make DESIRE a regulated drug (§9.5).
- The Panera case and victims' names: legal and platform risk. The villain works as a mechanism without them (§7.5).
- L-tyrosine benefits, flavor-duration claims, onset claims: unsupported or not yet measured (§8.3, §10.H).
- “No crash, no jitters”: saturated (§6.3).
